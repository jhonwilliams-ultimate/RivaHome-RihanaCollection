Highlights
📣
403,580 people reached on a $500 budget
That is roughly 807 people reached for every $1 — the full lifetime budget was delivered in 6 days.
💸
Very cheap exposure
CPM of $0.72 and $1.24 per 1,000 people reached — an efficient way to put the Rihana Collection in front of Kuwait.
🎯
2.39x return on Sales spend
27 purchases generated $2,632.31 against $1,099.97 spent, at an average purchase value of $97.49.
🛒
532 add-to-carts at $2.07 each
Strong shopping intent — 3,948 content views turned into 532 add-to-carts, a 13.5% rate.
✅
37.5% of checkouts became purchases
Of 72 shoppers who started checkout, 27 completed their purchase — people who reach checkout buy.
⏳
Purchases kept coming after ads stopped
9 of the 27 purchases were dated 2–5 Nov, after ads ended on 1 Nov — credited through the 7-day click window.
